# AI Prompt Sheet for SEO (always verify the output)

Replace the words in [brackets].

1. **Keyword ideas**
   "Act as an SEO specialist in Pakistan. List 30 search queries people in [city] might type when looking for [product/service]. Mix short and long-tail queries, include Roman Urdu variants where common, and group them by search intent (informational, commercial, transactional)."

2. **Clustering**
   "Group these keywords into clusters where one page could rank for all keywords in a cluster. Suggest a URL slug for each cluster: [paste list]."

3. **Content outline**
   "Create an H1/H2/H3 outline for a page targeting '[main keyword]' for [persona]. Include a section that answers the main question in under 50 words, a pricing section in PKR, and an FAQ section."

4. **Title and meta options**
   "Write 5 title tags (max 60 characters) and 5 meta descriptions (max 155 characters) for a page about [topic] in [city]. Include a clear benefit and call to action."

5. **FAQ ideas**
   "List 10 questions customers ask before buying [product/service] in Pakistan. Keep each question under 12 words."

6. **Content gap**
   "Here are the headings of the top 3 ranking pages for '[keyword]': [paste]. What important sub-topics are missing that would make a more helpful page?"

## Human review checklist
- [ ] Facts, prices and dates checked against real sources
- [ ] Added first-hand experience, photos or examples
- [ ] Removed generic filler sentences
- [ ] Brand voice and local language checked
- [ ] Keyword volumes verified in a real tool
