![SEO](https://img.shields.io/badge/Topic-SEO-4285F4?logo=google&logoColor=white) ![Search Console](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tool-Search_Console-458CF5?logo=googlesearchconsole&logoColor=white) ![Schema.org](https://img.shields.io/badge/Markup-Schema.org_JSON--LD-orange) ![AI](https://img.shields.io/badge/AI-Assisted_SEO-8A2BE2) ![Level](https://img.shields.io/badge/Level-Beginner-2EA44F)

> Diploma in Digital Marketing · Term 1 · 6 sessions × 2 hours · [⬅ Term Home](../README.md)

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) brings free, high-intent visitors from Google. This module explains how search engines crawl, index and rank pages, then teaches on-page, off-page, mobile, local, technical and voice SEO, and finishes with how AI and machine learning are changing search and how to use AI tools responsibly. Concept sessions alternate with practice labs where you research keywords, audit real websites and build an SEO-ready page.

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Explain crawling, indexing, ranking and the parts of a search engine
- Research keywords, map them to pages and optimise on-page elements
- Earn backlinks and use social media to support SEO
- Apply mobile, local and voice search optimisation
- Fix technical SEO: architecture, URLs, sitemaps, robots.txt, speed, HTTPS and structured data
- Use AI and NLP concepts to plan content and improve UX

## 📋 Prerequisites

- Module 01 (Foundations of Digital Marketing): personas and customer journey
- Basic web browsing; HTML knowledge helps but is not required (it is taught in Module 03)

## 🚀 Module Mini-Project

**SEO Starter Pack for a Local Business:**

1. Keyword research sheet: 40+ keywords, intent labels, 5 clusters mapped to URLs
2. On-page plans (title, meta, H1, H2s, alt text, internal links) for 3 pages
3. Technical kit: robots.txt, sitemap.xml, LocalBusiness and FAQ JSON-LD
4. Technical audit of the current site (or a competitor) with a prioritised action plan
5. One complete SEO page built from a content brief with AI assistance and human editing
6. A measurement plan using Search Console and GA4
