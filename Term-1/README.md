> Diploma in Digital Marketing · Term 1 · 82 sessions × 2 hours (theory + online practice) · [🏠 Repository Home](../README.md)

Term 1 takes you from "what is marketing?" to running and measuring real campaigns. You start with marketing foundations, learn to build and optimise websites, then work channel by channel: search engines (SEO and Google Ads), social media, email and mobile. You finish with AI tools and analytics, and bring it all together in a capstone campaign. Examples use Pakistani brands, platforms and PKR budgets so you can apply each idea straight away.

## 🎯 Term Learning Outcomes

By the end of Term 1 you will be able to:

- Explain core marketing ideas (4Ps/7Ps, STP, USP, customer journey, AIDA) and apply them to digital channels
- Optimise a website for search engines with on-page, off-page, technical, local and voice SEO techniques
- Build a well-structured, responsive website with HTML5, CSS3 and basic JavaScript
- Plan, create and measure Google search and display campaigns
- Manage social media presence, advertising, listening, YouTube and ROI measurement
- Plan, send and measure email campaigns that comply with consent rules
- Use mobile channels: SMS, QR codes, push, location-based and WhatsApp marketing
- Use AI tools responsibly for research, personalisation, content and automation
- Turn marketing data into decisions with GA4, Excel and Looker Studio dashboards
- Deliver a complete real-world campaign as a portfolio project

## 🧭 How to Study Each Session

1. Read the **Learning Objectives** so you know what to look for.
2. Study **Concepts Explained**; draw the diagrams in your notebook.
3. Follow the **Practical Walkthrough / Code Examples** on your own computer. Templates and files are in the same folder as the notes.
4. Complete the **Lab Practice** tasks, then the **Assignment**.
5. Test yourself with the **Quick Quiz** before opening the answers.
6. Practise the **Interview Questions** out loud; they are common in agency interviews.
7. Keep every assignment in one folder or Google Drive: by the end of the term it becomes your **portfolio**.

## 🧰 Free Accounts to Create Early

Google account (Gmail, Search Console, Analytics, Ads, Looker Studio), Meta Business Suite, Canva, WordPress.com, a free email marketing platform (for example Mailchimp or Brevo) and a code editor such as VS Code.
