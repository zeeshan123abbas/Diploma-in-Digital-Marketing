# Session 02: Practice Lab: Marketing Mix, USP and STP

> Module: Foundations of Digital Marketing · Diploma in Digital Marketing · Term 1 · ⏱ ~2 hours

This is a hands-on session. You will take the theory from Session 01 and apply it to a real-style business case: a Lahore-based online clothing start-up. By the end you will have a complete marketing foundation document that you could show to a client.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Analyse a business case and identify its marketing problems
- Build a complete 7P marketing mix for a product-based online business
- Segment a market using four segmentation bases
- Select a target segment using clear criteria
- Write a USP and a positioning statement and test them against competitors
- Draw a simple positioning map

## 📖 Concepts Explained

### 1. The case: "Rang-e-Lawn" (fictional)

Rang-e-Lawn is a new online brand selling unstitched lawn suits. It started six months ago on Instagram.

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Price range | PKR 3,500 to 6,500 per 3-piece suit |
| Sales channel | Instagram DMs and WhatsApp only |
| Monthly sales | about 80 suits |
| Main complaints | late replies, no size guide, no return policy |
| Competitors | big brands (higher price, malls) and hundreds of small Instagram sellers |

The owner asks: "How do we stand out and double sales without a big budget?"

### 2. Evaluating a segment: the SMART-R check

Before targeting a segment, check that it is:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| **S**ubstantial | Is it big enough to be profitable? |
| **M**easurable | Can we estimate its size and buying power? |
| **A**ccessible | Can we reach it with our channels (Instagram, WhatsApp, Google)? |
| **R**esponsive | Will it react to our offer differently from other segments? |
| **T**imely | Is now the right season (pre-Eid, summer launch)? |

### 3. Positioning map

A **positioning map** (perceptual map) places brands on two axes that customers care about, for example *price* (low to high) and *design* (basic to designer). Empty spaces on the map show opportunities.

```mermaid
quadrantChart
  title Lawn brands: price vs design
  x-axis Low price --> High price
  y-axis Basic design --> Designer look
  quadrant-1 Premium designer
  quadrant-2 Affordable designer (gap)
  quadrant-3 Budget basics
  quadrant-4 Overpriced basics
  Big mall brands: [0.8, 0.8]
  Small Instagram sellers: [0.25, 0.3]
  Rang-e-Lawn target: [0.4, 0.7]
```

The top-left quadrant (designer look at a fair price) is the gap Rang-e-Lawn can own.

### 4. Testing a USP

Ask three questions about any USP:

1. **Is it unique?** Could a competitor say the exact same thing?
2. **Is it valuable?** Does the customer care?
3. **Is it provable?** Can we show evidence (reviews, guarantee, photos)?

## 💻 Practical Walkthrough

Use the template [`case-worksheet.md`](./case-worksheet.md) in this folder. Follow these steps in order.

**Step 1: Diagnose problems with the 7Ps**

| P | Current state | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | Good fabric, 12 designs | No size/fabric guide | Add fabric details and stitching size chart |
| Price | PKR 3,500 to 6,500 | Fine | Add a bundle: 2 suits for PKR 9,000 |
| Place | Instagram DM only | Slow, no checkout | Add a simple Shopify or WooCommerce store |
| Promotion | Random posts | No plan | Weekly reels + micro-influencers |
| People | Owner replies alone | Late replies | WhatsApp Business quick replies, 1 helper |
| Process | Manual bank transfer | Drop-offs | Add cash on delivery |
| Physical evidence | No reviews shown | Low trust | Highlight customer photos and reviews |

**Step 2: Segment the market**

- Demographic: women 22 to 40, middle income
- Geographic: Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad first; COD makes other cities possible
- Psychographic: like designer looks, budget-conscious, shop on phone
- Behavioural: buy before Eid and summer launches; follow fashion pages

**Step 3: Choose the target** - "Working women 24 to 35 in big cities who want a designer look under PKR 6,000." It passes the SMART-R check.

**Step 4: Write USP and positioning**

- USP: "Designer-inspired lawn under PKR 6,000, delivered with free returns in 7 days."
- Positioning: For style-conscious working women, Rang-e-Lawn is the online lawn brand that gives a designer look at a fair price, because we design in-house and sell directly with no mall costs.

**How it works:** the case moves from problems (diagnosis) to a choice (target) to a message (USP/positioning). This is the same logic agencies use with real clients.

## 🧪 Lab Practice

1. Read the case and list at least five problems you see.
2. Fill the 7P diagnosis table in your own words with your own fixes.
3. Create four segments and score each one 1 to 5 on the SMART-R criteria.
4. Draw a positioning map (paper or Canva) with at least four competitor brands you know.
5. Write two USP options and test both with the three USP questions. Keep the stronger one.

## 💼 Interview Questions

**1. A client says "everyone is our customer". How do you respond?**

> Explain that a small budget spread over everyone reaches no one well. Show segments, score them, and recommend starting with the most responsive one.

**2. How do you find a positioning gap?**

> Map competitors on two attributes customers care about, look for empty quadrants, and check that real customers want what that gap offers.

**3. What makes a USP weak?**

> When it is generic ("best quality"), cannot be proven, or any competitor could say the same thing.

## ✅ Summary

- A case study is solved in order: diagnose → segment → target → position.
- The 7P table turns vague problems into specific fixes.
- SMART-R helps you choose a segment with reasons, not guesses.
- A positioning map reveals gaps in the market.
- Test every USP: unique, valuable, provable.
