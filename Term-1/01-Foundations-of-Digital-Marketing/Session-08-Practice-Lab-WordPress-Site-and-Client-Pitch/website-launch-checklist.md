# Website Launch Checklist (score 1 point per item)

## Message
- [ ] Hero headline states the USP clearly
- [ ] A visitor understands the offer in 5 seconds
- [ ] Each page has one clear call to action
- [ ] Prices or "starting from" price shown (PKR)

## Trust
- [ ] At least 3 testimonials or reviews
- [ ] Real photos of the business/team/products
- [ ] Address, phone and email visible in the footer
- [ ] About page tells the story of the business

## Conversion
- [ ] WhatsApp click-to-chat button works on mobile
- [ ] Contact form sends a test message successfully
- [ ] Phone number is clickable on mobile
- [ ] FAQ answers top objections

## Technical
- [ ] Looks good on mobile (tested on a real phone)
- [ ] Site opens with https (padlock)
- [ ] Images are compressed (each under ~300 KB)
- [ ] No broken links or empty pages
- [ ] Privacy Policy page exists

## SEO basics
- [ ] One H1 heading per page
- [ ] Page titles describe the page and location (e.g. "Chai Café in North Nazimabad")
- [ ] Image alt text added
