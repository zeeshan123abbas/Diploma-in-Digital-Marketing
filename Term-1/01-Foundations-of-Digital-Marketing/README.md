> Diploma in Digital Marketing · Term 1 · 8 sessions × 2 hours · [⬅ Term Home](../README.md)

Before you run an ad or write a hashtag, you need to think like a marketer. This module teaches the classic ideas that every digital campaign is built on: the marketing process, the 4Ps and 7Ps, consumer behaviour, USP and STP, the customer journey and AIDA, buyer personas, the role of digital media, presentation skills and a first WordPress website. Every concept comes with Pakistani business examples and a ready-to-use template.

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Explain marketing basics, the marketing process and consumer behaviour
- Apply the 4Ps/7Ps, write a USP and use Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning
- Compare traditional and digital marketing and name the types of digital marketing
- Map a customer journey, write AIDA messages and build buyer personas
- Classify paid, owned and earned media and plan a channel mix with a PKR budget
- Present ideas persuasively and build a basic business website in WordPress

## 📋 Prerequisites

- Basic computer and internet skills
- A Google account (for Forms, Sheets and Slides)
- Curiosity about how brands you use every day attract you

## 🚀 Module Mini-Project

**Marketing Foundation Kit for a Local Business** - pick a real small business in your area (with the owner's permission if possible):

1. 7P marketing mix and three scored customer segments
2. USP, positioning statement and a positioning map with 4 competitors
3. Two buyer personas built from at least 5 short interviews or survey responses
4. A customer journey map with touchpoints, emotions and fixes
5. A one-month media mix plan with a PKR budget and KPIs
6. A free WordPress site (home, about, services, contact) with a WhatsApp CTA
7. A 6 to 8 slide pitch deck presented in 5 minutes
